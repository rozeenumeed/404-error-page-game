🎮 How to Play

Memory Match Game

🃏

1. Click a Card

Click any card to reveal the hidden emoji.

👀

2. Find a Matching Pair

Click a second card and try to find the same emoji.

✅

3. Match the Cards

If both emojis match, the cards stay open. Otherwise, they flip back after a short delay.

🏆

4. Complete the Game

Find every matching pair using your memory. Try to finish in as few moves as possible!
